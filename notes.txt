The daemon in this project
In gochi, the daemon is the process that:

Owns the serial port to your ESP32 board (only one program can hold it at a time)
Stays running so the desk pet keeps working even when you close your terminal
Listens for commands (from the CLI, or the HTTP server) and forwards them to the board

sudo launchctl stop com.tamagotchi.daemon
Tell macOS to shut down the gochi background helper that runs your desk pet.
